Label these — the one thing only you can do

Each of these is a finding from the ledger. The question is whether it is a fact about the game — Sin & Punishment’s own code, data or behaviour — or not. That is your own question: is this game unusually awkward, or are we just being slow.

“Not the game” is a complete answer, not a shrug. The two faint buttons after it — stack, method — are optional detail if you happen to know which; the tools that run it, or the way this project works. They are genuinely hard to separate and you should not force it: when I configure a tool, the tool’s capability is stack and my choice of setting is method, and they arrive as one fact in one sentence.

Why it has to be you. I already have an answer key, but it is my reading. Two independent runs scored 88% and 100% against it, which measures agreement with me, not correctness. Yours is the first that would be a real key. A99 is the one that matters — it is the crash that dominated this project, and it is the entry no two readers have labelled the same way. Partial is genuinely fine; one label is worth more than none.

My labels are deliberately not shown, and neither are the two machine runs — a page that showed you my answer would collect agreement rather than a judgement. Go with your gut; there is no penalty for a wrong one, and a disagreement is a more useful result than a match.
